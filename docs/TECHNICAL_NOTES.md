# Technical qualifications

These notes accompany the translated report and do not change historical simulation results.

- The source uses both initial attenuation and late-time amplification to describe the response. Quantitative comparisons need raw histories, defined windows, and a consistent metric.
- The source prose gives an amplitude in meters, but the shown geometry and property entries suggest a millimeter-based model. Confirm original units before quoting an amplitude.
- Fixed outer edges generate reflections. Late-time peaks do not alone prove resonance, creeping-wave behavior, or a unique scattering mechanism.
- The report names symmetric and antisymmetric Lamb modes but supplies no mode separation or dispersion calculation. Do not claim a specific mode was verified.
- No mesh or time-step convergence study, experimental validation, detection threshold, localization algorithm, or sensor-placement optimization is supplied.
- The small-hole radius and exact element designation are unavailable. Do not estimate them from screenshots as if they were original inputs.
- The tabular pulse is documented; a general discussion of Hanning-windowed pulses does not establish one was applied.
- This is Group 11 work. The report lists Pouya Asghari, Mehdi Gharibshahi, and Arash Bakhtiari without individual task allocation.
- Rose's first bibliography entry has been explicitly corrected from the source year 2014 to the publisher's first-publication year 1999. Publisher evidence: https://assets.cambridge.org/97805215/48892/frontmatter/9780521548892_frontmatter.pdf
